# About Aaditya Properties

Aaditya Properties is an independent publication of guides to property in Gujarat. We explain the official processes, documents, costs and rules involved in buying, selling, owning and renting property, in plain English.

{% if site.operator_name != "" %}The site is published by **{{ site.operator_name }}**{% if site.operator_city != "" %}, based in {{ site.operator_city }}{% endif %}.{% endif %}

## What we do
- Explain official procedures step by step, such as viewing land records on AnyROR, looking up jantri values on GARVI, and checking projects on GujRERA.
- Link every rule, rate and threshold to its official source, with the date we checked it.
- Show our working whenever we use numbers.
- Correct mistakes openly. See our [corrections policy]({{ '/corrections/' | relative_url }}).

## What we don't do
- We do not list or sell properties, and we don't promote builders or projects.
- We do not predict property prices or promise investment returns.
- We do not give personal legal, tax or investment advice. For decisions involving significant money or legal risk, consult an advocate or chartered accountant.

## Who writes the guides
Guides are researched and written by the Aaditya Properties editorial desk. They are published under the desk's name until individual author profiles are added. We only say an article was "reviewed by" someone when a named, qualified person actually reviewed it. Read [how we work]({{ '/editorial-policy/' | relative_url }}).

## How the site is funded
The site may show advertising served by Google AdSense. Advertisers have no say in what we write. See our [advertising disclosure]({{ '/advertising-disclosure/' | relative_url }}).
