# Privacy policy

*Effective: 23 October 2026*

This policy explains what information is collected when you use this website and how it is used.

## Who we are
This website is operated by Aaditya Properties{% if site.operator_name != "" %} ({{ site.operator_name }}){% endif %}. You can reach us through our [contact page]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}).

## Information we collect
- **When you email us:** your email address and anything you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to correct our content, and we keep it no longer than needed.
- **Technical data:** like most websites, our hosting provider may record standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, time) for security and operation.
- **Fonts:** pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Search:** site search runs entirely in your browser; your search terms are not sent to us.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** used by advertising partners, as described below.

We have no user accounts or comment forms, and we don't sell personal information.

## Advertising (Google AdSense)
This site may show ads served by Google.
- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland will be asked for consent through a Google-certified consent message before personalised ads are shown.

## Your choices and rights
You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information you have sent us by contacting us. We handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## Children
This site is not directed at children under 18.

## Changes
If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above.
