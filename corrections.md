# Corrections policy

We want every guide to be accurate and current. When we get something wrong, we fix it and say so.

- **Report an error** through our [contact page]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}). Tell us the page, the statement and, if possible, the official source.
- We aim to acknowledge reports within two working days and correct verified errors within five working days. Errors in tax or legal figures are corrected as a priority.
- **How corrections appear:** we add a dated note at the end of the article explaining what changed. Substantive corrections also update the "Last updated" date.
- **Rule changes:** when an official rule changes (for example after the Union Budget or an RBI decision), we update the affected guides and note the change.
