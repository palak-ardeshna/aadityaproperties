How we work

Sources first

Rules and rates come from Acts, notifications and official portals such as GujRERA, GARVI, AnyROR, the Income Tax Department and RBI — linked next to each claim.

Shown working

Every worked example lists its assumptions, and calculations are recomputed independently before publishing.

Dated and corrected

Each guide states its jurisdiction and date. When rules change or we get something wrong, we update the guide and note what changed.
