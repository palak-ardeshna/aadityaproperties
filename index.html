How to Buy Your First Home in Gujarat: A Step-by-Step Guide

The order of steps for a first home purchase in Gujarat, covering budget, loan limits, legal checks, stamp duty, TDS and registration, with official sources.

By Aaditya Properties Editorial Desk · 23 Oct 2026 · 5 min read

In brief
Fix a budget that covers stamp duty, registration and other costs on top of the price. Check how much a bank can lend under RBI's loan-to-value limits. Verify the property's documents and, for new projects, its GujRERA registration before paying a token. Sign a written agreement, deduct 1% TDS if the price is ₹50 lakh or more, and register the sale deed at the sub-registrar office.
