How to Buy Your First Home in Gujarat: A Step-by-Step Guide

October 23, 2026 by Aaditya Properties Editorial Desk

Fix a budget that covers stamp duty, registration and other costs on top of the price. Check how much a bank can lend under RBI's loan-to-value limits. Verify the property's documents and, for new projects, its GujRERA registration before paying a token. Sign a written agreement, deduct 1% TDS if the price is ₹50 lakh or more, and register the sale deed at the sub-registrar office. Read more

Documents to Check Before Buying Property in Gujarat: A Practical Checklist

October 23, 2026 by Aaditya Properties Editorial Desk

Before paying any token amount in Gujarat, check the land record (7/12 and 8A, or the property card in city survey areas), the chain of title deeds, an encumbrance search on GARVI, the NA order for non-agricultural use, the approved plan and BU permission for buildings, and GujRERA registration for new projects. Most records can be viewed online on official portals, but a clean online record is not proof of clear title — have an advocate do a title search. Read more
