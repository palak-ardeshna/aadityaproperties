# Advertising disclosure

- This site may display advertising served by **Google AdSense**. Google chooses which ads to show; we do not choose individual advertisers.
- Ads are visually separate from our content and are never presented as guides or recommendations.
- Advertisers cannot pay to influence what we write, which topics we cover or how we describe any product.
- We do not currently publish sponsored articles or use affiliate links. If that changes, sponsored content will be labelled "Sponsored" at the top, and affiliate links will be disclosed on the page where they appear.
- Read about cookies used for advertising in our [privacy policy]({{ '/privacy-policy/' | relative_url }}).
