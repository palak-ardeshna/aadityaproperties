Property is a large, slow-moving and expensive purchase. Mistakes are hard to undo. Instead of a list of "tips", here are eight questions to answer honestly before you commit.

## 1. What is the property for?

Rental income and long-term holding need different properties:
- For **income**, what matters is realistic rent, tenant demand and low running costs.
- For **long-term holding**, location fundamentals and your ability to keep holding through vacancies or a weak market matter more.

Be wary of anyone who promises both, or names a specific future price.

## 2. What will it really cost to buy?

Add the price, stamp duty and registration (in Gujarat, check the official calculator on [GARVI](https://garvi.gujarat.gov.in/)), GST if the property is under construction, legal fees, loan fees, society charges and any brokerage. Our [first-home guide]({{ '/how-to-buy-your-first-home-in-india/' | relative_url }}) lists these.

## 3. What will it cost to hold?

Every year: property tax, maintenance or society charges, repairs, insurance, and months without a tenant. Then estimate the **net yield**:

**(annual rent − vacancy − annual costs) ÷ total purchase cost × 100**

Our [residential vs commercial guide]({{ '/residential-vs-commercial-property-investment/' | relative_url }}) has a worked example.

## 4. Can your finances survive a bad year?

If you borrow:
- Could you pay the EMI for six months with no rent?
- Could you still pay if the rate rose by one or two percentage points?

RBI raised the policy repo rate to 5.50% on 7 October 2026 ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63742)), and floating loan rates typically follow. RBI's lending limits mean a lender usually finances at most 75–90% of the property's value, depending on the loan size ([RBI Master Circular – Housing Finance](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasCirculardetails.aspx?id=12282)).

## 5. Is the title clean and the project registered?

Check the land record, title chain, encumbrances, NA order and BU permission. For new projects, check registration on the state RERA portal ([GujRERA](https://gujrera.gujarat.gov.in/) in Gujarat). Have an advocate do a title search. See the [document checklist]({{ '/documents-required-before-buying-a-property/' | relative_url }}).

## 6. How will the income and profit be taxed?

- **Rent** is taxed under "income from house property". A standard deduction of 30% of the annual value is allowed, plus interest on borrowed capital. This is section 22 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, formerly section 24 of the 1961 Act ([Income Tax Department](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-22-211)). How much of a loss you can set off depends on the tax regime you choose.
- **Profit on sale:** property held for more than 24 months is a long-term capital asset. For transfers on or after 23 July 2024, long-term gains are taxed at 12.5% without indexation. Resident individuals and HUFs who acquired the property before that date can compare this with 20% with indexation and pay the lower amount ([Income Tax Department](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/tax-on-long-term-capital-gains%E2%80%8B)).

Tax rules are detailed and change with each Budget. Consult a chartered accountant for your own situation.

## 7. How would you get out?

Property can take months to sell, and selling costs money: brokerage, and for the buyer, stamp duty, which affects the price they will pay. Ask how many similar units are for sale nearby and how long they have been listed. A market with a lot of unsold stock is slower to sell into.

## 8. Is too much of your wealth in one asset?

For many households one property is most of their net worth. Ask whether you'd still be comfortable if that property's value stayed flat for years, or if it sat empty for a while.

## A short checklist

- [ ] Goal defined: income or long-term holding
- [ ] Full purchase cost calculated
- [ ] Net yield estimated with vacancy and costs
- [ ] EMI stress-tested for a rate rise and six months without rent
- [ ] Title search by an advocate; RERA registration checked
- [ ] Tax position discussed with a chartered accountant
- [ ] Exit plan and realistic time to sell considered
